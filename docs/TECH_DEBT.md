# TECH_DEBT

## Purpose

Accepted debt is a known limitation with rationale, owner, risk, and trigger. A known limitation without rationale or trigger is unresolved drift, not accepted debt.

## Entry Format

```text
### TD-0001 - Short title
Status: latent | active | paid
Severity:
Owner:
Rationale:
Risk:
Trigger:
Disposition:
```

IDs are never reused. Paid items stay in the Paid section.
